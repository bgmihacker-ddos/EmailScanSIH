1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT (THE HOOK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* THE PROBLEM:
  Email remains the primary entry vector for over 90% of cyber breaches, Business
  Email Compromise (BEC), financial fraud, and credential harvesting. Attackers
  consistently bypass traditional rule-based spam filters using generative AI,
  homoglyph domain spoofing, deceptive display names, compromised relay chains,
  and proxy/TOR routing.

* THE CRITICAL GAP:
  Existing enterprise Secure Email Gateways (SEGs) act as opaque black boxes.
  They flag or drop emails without transparent explanations, provide zero hop-by-
  hop visual geographic mail-flow attribution, offer no tamper-evident chain of
  custody for digital evidence admissible in court, and ignore regional threat
  contexts (such as Indian banking impersonation and .gov.in spoofing).

* OUR SOLUTION:
  An end-to-end multi-layered forensic intelligence and detection platform that:
  - Ingests and statically dissects raw RFC 822 / MIME (.eml) files safely.
  - Performs independent cryptographic verification of SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and ARC.
  - Reconstructs and geographically visualizes the entire SMTP relay chain.
  - Leverages asynchronous threat intelligence and public infrastructure scanning.
  - Employs hybrid threat scoring with explainable AI (XAI) rationale.
  - Anchors forensic evidence onto an immutable blockchain ledger.
  - Exports court-ready PDF forensic dossiers and pre-formatted CERT-In reports.


2. TECHNICAL APPROACH (LAYER-BY-LAYER ARCHITECTURE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The architecture is structured across 5 distinct, decoupled pipeline stages:

[Raw Email (.eml)]
        |
        v
[STAGE 1: MULTI-PART PARSER & HEADER FORENSICS]
  * MIME Body & Base64 decoding; token-safe extraction preventing payload execution.
  * Independent cryptographic verification (SPF, DKIM, DMARC via dkimpy and pyspf).
  * Received hop traversal, header timing calculation, and timezone drift anomaly.
        |
        v
[STAGE 2: MULTIMODAL THREAT & CONTENT ANALYSIS]
  * Deceptive HTML parser and homoglyph / lookalike detector (Levenshtein distance).
  * NLP social engineering, urgency, and coercion marker detection (BEC patterns).
  * Attachment static profiler (OOXML inspection, double-extension, PDF token scans).
  * Dual-tier ML classification (TF-IDF + Linear Classifier / Transformer inference).
        |
        v
[STAGE 3: THREAT INTELLIGENCE & GEOIP ENRICHMENT]
  * Asynchronous multi-provider lookups (VirusTotal, URLhaus, AbuseIPDB, AlienVault OTX).
  * Public infrastructure fingerprinting (Shodan InternetDB, Spamhaus DNSBL, RDAP).
  * Hop-by-hop GeoTracing & anonymizer detection (ip-api, TOR exit node cache, VPN flags).
        |
        v
[STAGE 4: THREAT FUSION, ATTRIBUTION & REASONING]
  * Deduplication & normalization (EvidenceCorrelator and IOC Extractor).
  * Multi-factor risk scorer generating a calibrated 0-100 risk index.
  * Explainable AI (XAI) engine generating plain-language causal explanations.
  * MITRE ATT&CK technique matrix and cyber kill chain stage mapping.
        |
        v
[STAGE 5: FORENSIC PRESERVATION & AUTOMATED RESPONSE]
  * Immutable chain-of-custody (SHA-256 evidence hashing + Blockchain Ledger anchoring).
  * Interactive visualization (MapLibre relay path map & interactive evidence graph).
  * Incident response exports (PDF forensic dossiers, STIX 2.1 bundles, CERT-In templates).

Key Technical Hallmarks:
1. Zero-Execution Static Sandbox: Static parsing guarantees that malicious
   attachments and deceptive payloads are never executed on the host server.
2. Deterministic Fallbacks & Circuit Breakers: Network queries feature strict
   timeouts. If external APIs throttle, offline heuristic fallbacks maintain 100%
   uptime.
3. Telemetry & Sub-3-Second Performance: Built-in StageTimer logs latency per
   analytical phase, demonstrating sub-3-second end-to-end processing speeds.


3. RESEARCH FOUNDATIONS & REFERENCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Email Protocols & Security Specifications:
  * RFC 7208: Sender Policy Framework (SPF) for Authorizing IP Addresses.
  * RFC 6376: DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM) Signatures.
  * RFC 7489: Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance (DMARC).
  * RFC 8617: The Authenticated Received Chain (ARC) Protocol.
  * RFC 5322: Internet Message Format (IMF) structural standards.

B. Threat Frameworks & Standardization:
  * MITRE ATT&CK Matrix for Enterprise:
    - T1566.001 / T1566.002: Phishing (Spearphishing Attachment & Spearphishing Link).
    - T1078: Valid Accounts (Credential harvesting via deceptive portals).
    - T1036: Masquerading (Homoglyph and display-name spoofing).
    - T1204: User Execution (Malicious files / links).
  * OASIS STIX 2.1 Specification: Standardized cyber threat intelligence packaging.
  * ISO/IEC 27037: Guidelines for identification, collection, acquisition, and
    preservation of digital evidence.

C. Datasets & Literature:
  * Enron Email Dataset & SpamAssassin Public Corpus (Model benchmarking baseline).
  * Deception detection research in Business Email Compromise (linguistic markers
    of urgency, authority impersonation, and payment diversion).


4. FEASIBILITY & VIABILITY ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY:
   * Status: High and Proven.
   * Architecture utilizes FastAPI with asynchronous Python coroutines, a React +
     TypeScript dashboard, and MapLibre GL for spatial rendering.
   * Runs natively on commodity hardware and standard cloud instances (Linux/Docker)
     without requiring expensive proprietary sandboxing hardware.

2. FINANCIAL VIABILITY (COST MODEL):
   * Status: Exceptional.
   * Primary pipeline uses open-source libraries (pyspf, dkimpy, weasyprint).
   * Geolocation and threat intelligence utilize community free tiers and keyless
     endpoints (Shodan InternetDB, AbuseIPDB, URLhaus, TOR bulk lists, RDAP).
   * Operational cost per scan is effectively zero under standard operational loads.

3. OPERATIONAL FEASIBILITY & ADOPTION:
   * Status: Frictionless.
   * Does not require organizations to abandon their existing email infrastructure.
   * Functions as an API gateway, standalone web workbench for SOC analysts, or
     an integrated hook for IMAP/Exchange mailflow rules.

4. LEGAL & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE:
   * Status: Law-Enforcement and Court Ready.
   * Automatic PII redaction and payload masking ensure DPDP Act / GDPR compliance.
   * SHA-256 evidence hashing and immutable blockchain timestamping prevent claim
     of post-incident tampering during legal proceedings.
