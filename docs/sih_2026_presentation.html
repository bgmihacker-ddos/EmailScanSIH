Slide 02 · Problem Framing

The Growing Menace of Advanced Email Fraud

🚨 The Threat Landscape
  • • 90%+ Cyber Attacks Start via Email: Spear-phishing, Business Email Compromise (BEC), credential theft, and ransomware deployment.
  • • Generative AI Evasion: Attackers craft grammatically flawless, highly contextual urgency lures that bypass classical keyword filters.
  • • Infrastructure Obfuscation: Use of lookalike homoglyph domains, residential proxies, and bulletproof relay servers.
⚠️ Critical Gaps in Current Solutions
  • • Opaque Black-Box Verdicts: Standard gateways mark emails as "Spam" without explaining why, hindering forensic triage.
  • • No Visual Relay Attribution: Zero hop-by-hop geographic mapping of the actual SMTP transfer path.
  • • Lack of Chain-of-Custody: Digital evidence is easily challenged in court due to lack of tamper-proof verification.
  • • Ignored Regional Context: No native detection for Indian banking (UPI, SBI, HDFC) or government domain (.gov.in) spoofing.
Core Research Finding: Detection must shift from simple content filtering to Triangulated Threat Fusion—verifying Cryptographic Identity, Physical Infrastructure Origin, and Behavioral Intent simultaneously.

2. Infrastructure Attribution

Dissects the RFC 5322 Received hops back to originating IP. Enriches each hop with multi-provider Geolocation, Shodan open ports, Tor exit node lists, and RDAP domain age.

3. Behavioral NLP & ML

Static sandbox inspection of attachments (macros/OLE streams), homoglyph/typosquatting detection, social engineering lexical cues, and an ensemble TF-IDF + DistilBERT classifier.

Scikit-Learn · DistilBERT · Safe Static Parser
💡 The Secret Sauce: If an email passes SPF but originates from an unauthorized Tor node, or features clean text but a 3-day-old homoglyph domain, the Threat Fusion Engine triggers an immediate high-severity quarantine.

Tamper-Proof Chain-of-Custody on Ethereum

The Problem with Digital Evidence in Court

In electronic fraud prosecution under the Indian Information Technology Act, defense attorneys routinely challenge email evidence, claiming logs were altered, timestamps backdated, or headers forged after discovery.

Our Immutable Blockchain Anchor

Upon scanning, our engine calculates the SHA-256 cryptographic digest of the raw `.eml` and analysis manifest, committing it directly to a Solidity smart contract on the Ethereum Sepolia Testnet.
